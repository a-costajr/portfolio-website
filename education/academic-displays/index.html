Memory Forensic Investigation

This documents outlines an investigation of a memory image that was conducted as part of one of the many labs throughout the semester. Here, we leveraged Volatility 2 & 3 to discover malicious processes and their executables in memory, while also partially reconstructing the filesystem to locate images that were deemed by the lab instruction as evidence. Ultimately, this project led to our initial interest in developing our own plugin that scans for HiddenPE files using entropy-based filtering. I would like to extend a special thank you to another close friend Christopher Protheroe who I had the pleasure of working with on multiple occasions.

KGL Penetration Test Report

This document outlines an extensive, semester long penetration testing report that was conducted by our group. The report was multi-faceted, and included a variety of different methods to accomplish our goals in the context of the predefined engagement rules. This report was written in the context of providing a high-level, but detailed report that can be provided to stakeholders, and includes multiple graphics and hard evidence, such as CVSS 3.1 scoring and charts displaying identified vulnerabilities.

Reverse Engineering WannaCry Ransomware

This document provides a high-level deconstruction of the notorious WannaCry ransomware. Leveraging Ghidra, we were able to analyze both the assembly code of the executable in a sandboxed environment to determine the exact entry point of the attack, as well as the RSA key pair and AES key that was used in the encryption aspect of the attack. A special thank you once again to Christopher Protheroe, who worked alongside me through the project while we had the flu.
