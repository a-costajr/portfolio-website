NSA Codebreaker Challenge

The NSA Codebreaker Challenge consists of a series of tasks that are worth a varying number of points based upon their difficulty. Schools across the country are ranked according to the total number of points accumulated by participating students. I have participated in both the 2024 and 2025 challenge, where our school places very high each year.
