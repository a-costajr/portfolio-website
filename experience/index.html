UCPO - Cyber Crimes & High-Tech Internship

On June 3, 2024, I was very fortunate to begin my first internship with the Union County Prosecutor’s Office as an intern within the Cyber Crime & High-Tech Unit. Throughout the course of my internship, I made significant strides in advancing my technical skills as a cybersecurity professional while also establishing strong professional relationships with my coworkers and members of the community.

During my time with the unit, I assisted in ongoing criminal investigations by conducting forensic examinations on digital devices, adhering to structured investigative methodologies, and maintaining proper chain-of-custody procedures. I was trained in the preparation and certification of legal documentation, including subpoenas, consent forms, and search warrants, and contributed to investigations involving identity theft, cyber-enabled theft, and the possession or distribution of child exploitation material in coordination with NCMEC. I gained hands-on experience with computer forensics and high-tech investigative tools, collaborated with Assistant Prosecutors on evidence development and evaluation, and supported surveillance and communication initiatives such as pole cameras and wiretaps. Additionally, I performed over 50 OSINT-based analyses for suspect identification, attended law enforcement training initiatives and ride-alongs, participated in statistical and operational briefings with agency leadership, and engaged in community outreach efforts aimed at supporting individuals affected by substance abuse.
